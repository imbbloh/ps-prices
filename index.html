How it works: the title is sent to your Render backend, which reads each region's official PlayStation Store price; this page then converts to your chosen currency using live rates (open.er-api.com). A ⚠ marks a store listing in an unexpected currency (e.g. Mexico in USD).
Note: buying a cheaper region's price needs a PSN account set to that region + its gift-card credit.
